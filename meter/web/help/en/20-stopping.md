# Using Farever Book

> A single window, on your second screen, that never touches the game.

Farever Book reads Farever's combat data and shows it in this window. Nothing
is ever drawn in the game: your HUD stays exactly Farever's.

## With or without the game

Launch Farever Book whenever you like, before or after the game. The light at
the top right shows where it stands:

* **● Offline**: Farever is not running. Rifts, fight history and settings
  can still be read.
* **● Connecting…**: the game was just detected, Farever Book is hooking in.
* **● In game**: the data arrives live.
* **● Failed, retry**: click the light for a new attempt.

When you close the game, Farever Book goes back to "Offline" and reconnects
on its own at the next session.

## The pages

* **In game**: the damage and healing meter, the details of the selected
  player (click a row of the meter) and the events: boss kills, records, rift
  ends.
* **Rifts**: rift loot (portal tiers, boss chest, bonus chest), then every
  finished rift, day by day, with its full ranking.
* **Dungeons**: each dungeon, with the history of your runs: difficulty,
  result (victory, failure, abandoned), time, deaths, group, and the full
  report of each run (exploration then boss), with the loot picked up and the
  content of the final chest. The records per difficulty are marked with a
  star.

* **Collection**: your mounts, gliders and companions, gear appearances
  (armor, filterable by slot) and the Codex items (components, ores, cloth,
  leather) with how many you got and their rank. Farever Book reads it on its
  own while the game is open and keeps it to browse with the game closed.
  Click an item to see how to get it (loot, factions, gathering, crafting,
  salvaging…).

* **Codex**: your hunting log: how many times your character killed each
  monster, according to the game's Codex, and the rank reached.

* **Map**: the world map with chests, red orbs, obelisks and respawn points.
  Drag to move, scroll to zoom, click a point for its details.

The rift timer is always visible in the header: it counts down to the next
rift (they open every hour on the hour), then the 3 minutes during which the
portal stays open.

## Reset mid-fight

The keyboard shortcut (**Shift + \** by default) resets the fight without
taking your eyes off the game. It only works while Farever is in the
foreground, shows nothing in the game, and can be changed in **Settings**.

## Stopping Farever Book

Just close this window (the cross at the top right), or **right-click the
icon** near the clock → **Stop the meter**. Either way, Farever Book detaches
cleanly from the game.

**Do not stop it from the Task Manager**: that kills the process before it
can detach from the game, and that is what can destabilize Farever.
